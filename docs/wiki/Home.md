## What's included?

- **Aquariums:** Connected glass water tanks with swimming fish, axolotls, mini squid/glow squid, baby turtles and rare small drowned.
- **Swim Tubes:** Branching links between tanks. Their water is contained, and they do not add creature capacity.
- **Terrariums:** Separate passive and hostile networks, with matching pipes, dry chambers and shallow decorative water patches.
- **Mob Nets:** Reusable capture and release tools preserving individual mob data.
- **Mobitat:** Up to **five mobs of the same type** in one portable block; placed blocks render animated miniature inhabitants.
- **Decorating:** Add multiple items/blocks to ceiling, body or floor, and independently move, rotate or scale them using the editor.
- **Tropical Fish Editor:** A dedicated one-fish tank for editing all 12 vanilla pattern choices and 16 colors for each color channel.
